Add `SolverCoupledADMM.ContactPair(full_surface=True)`: rigid shapes are coupled to the edges and triangle interiors of the other entry's soft meshes, not only to its particles, with each edge/face contact impulse distributed over the feature's particles by barycentric weight. This stops thin rigid features (e.g. gripper pads) passing between cloth particles. Edge/face records already implied by the particles' own contacts are dropped (`Config.full_surface_redundancy_tolerance`); not yet supported with `gamma > 0`.
